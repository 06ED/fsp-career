# Assessment and matching validation protocol

**Status: the candidate assessment has a synthetic validation study with reported results (below); matching and FSP parts remain a protocol, not a validation claim.** The requirements call for a candidate assessment grade that cannot be forcibly downgraded, a time-limited grade change, ranking informed by verified achievements, and evidence of expert validity, discrimination, reliability and top-k pair quality. The requirements PDF was provided separately from this checkout; this document uses its extracted requirements summary. The following synthetic expectations are **test oracles**, not measured outcomes or claims about real candidates/FSP credentials.

## Candidate assessment: mechanism and results

**Results: [`validation/assessment-results.md`](validation/assessment-results.md)** (Russian report, regenerated by `cd apps/api && gradle validateAssessment`; raw numbers in [`assessment-results.json`](validation/assessment-results.json)). The study simulates 10 000 synthetic candidates through the production engine and compares it with the previous five-question test on the same simulees: accuracy against reference labels, macro-F1 and confusion matrix; test–retest agreement on independently generated item instances (weighted kappa, θ correlation); per-family p-values, item–rest correlations and information; conditional bias/SD of θ̂ across forms; instance overlap between candidates and a memorization attack with leaks of 200/1 000/5 000 served questions; sensitivity to the decision margin, imperfect isomorphism and test length. Item parameters are expert-assigned priors, so the numbers describe the mechanism under its assumptions; section 9 of the report gives the calibration plan (pilot, IRT fit, instance variance, expert-labelled sample, drift and leak monitoring).

- **Content** ([`assessment/content`](../apps/api/src/main/kotlin/ru/fsp/career/assessment/content)): item *families* — one skill at one calibrated difficulty with 2PL parameters (*a*, *b*; multiple choice adds a fixed guessing floor *c* = 1/options). Most families are parametric generators (SQL over generated tables, code tracing in Kotlin/Python/JS, event loop, CSS specificity, statistics, unit economics, WCAG contrast, …); the rest are sets of equivalent scenario variants. Every attempt gets its own instance drawn from a CSPRNG seed; the key is stored only in `assessment_items` and never serialized. The legacy `questions` bank (V1/V2) is no longer served.
- **Test** ([`Cat.kt`](../apps/api/src/main/kotlin/ru/fsp/career/assessment/Cat.kt), [`Irt.kt`](../apps/api/src/main/kotlin/ru/fsp/career/assessment/Irt.kt)): survey (industry, specialization, experience, team roles, work format) → declared grade → 12 adaptive items in 30 minutes. Item choice: maximum Fisher information at the routing estimate within topic quotas of the blueprint, least-exposed among near-equal candidates. Scoring: EAP with N(0, 1) prior (the declaration only routes the test). Cut-points on θ: middle −0.5, senior +0.8 (junior entry −1.5); a declared grade is confirmed when θ̂ − k·SE ≥ cut (k chosen by the sweep in the report); score = 100·Φ(θ̂). An expired attempt counts unanswered items as wrong and never confirms a grade.
- **Grade rules** ([`RetakePolicy.kt`](../apps/api/src/main/kotlin/ru/fsp/career/assessment/RetakePolicy.kt)): confirmed grade = max(previous, declared if passed), first failure floors at junior, never lowered. Per specialization, one grade change per 90 days; immediate exceptions: after a failure one attempt one level lower, after a confident pass one attempt one level higher (not if that level was failed in the window). Enforced at attempt start (`409` with `retakeAvailableAt`).
- **Vacancy blueprint** ([`Blueprint.kt`](../apps/api/src/main/kotlin/ru/fsp/career/assessment/Blueprint.kt)): ru/en skill dictionary with light stemming turns a vacancy text into topic weights (60 % vacancy, 40 % specialization core) and item quotas; `POST /api/assessments/blueprint` shows skills, quotas and freshly generated sample items for the five demo vacancies or any text.
- **Checks run**: unit tests for every generator (determinism, option uniqueness, keys re-derived from rendered text by independent oracles on hundreds of seeds), IRT/EAP, retake rules, blueprint and CAT selection; a DB test runs survey → failed senior → immediate middle → retake blocks → expiry against PostgreSQL. [`smoke_assessment.py`](smoke_assessment.py) repeats that path over HTTP against the running stack.

## What exists now and what was actually checked (matching, profile, invitations)

- V2 requires `processingConsent=true` separately from `consentPublication=true` to store a profile and start an assessment; matches require both consents, a nonblank full name, same specialization, a completed assessment and assessed grade meeting the effective minimum. `DELETE /api/candidates/me/consent` clears profile fields and erases the candidate's attempts/answers, achievements, resume drafts and invitations while leaving an empty account/profile shell. `GET /api/assessments/history` lists the candidate's completed attempts with survey, declared grade, θ, SE, score and confirmed grade, newest first. [`ResumeRoutes.kt`](../apps/api/src/main/kotlin/ru/fsp/career/profiles/http/ResumeRoutes.kt) accepts one text-extractable PDF at most 5 MB/30 pages with processing consent, stores a suggested-fields draft rather than directly applying parsed content, then requires candidate review/confirmation within one day; confirmation updates selected profile fields, never verified email or publication consent. Do not upload real resumes for the synthetic protocol. Branded PDF export is generated in the browser from a saved profile with pdfmake and the current UI theme; [`resume-pdf.test.ts`](../apps/web/test/resume-pdf.test.ts) checks its data, embedded fonts and pagination.
- **Matching (V6).** `GET /api/matches` (need-based selection) and `GET /api/bank/candidates` (whole bank) consider only published candidates (processing + publication consent, nonblank name) with an **assessed category** (specialization + best assessed grade in the current specialization); self-declared `desiredGrade`, `grade`/`score` in profile JSON and listed skills can never create or raise a category. The pure engine lives in [`matching/MatchingEngine.kt`](../apps/api/src/main/kotlin/ru/fsp/career/matching/MatchingEngine.kt) (formula and weights documented in the header comment): grade fit exact ×1.00, one higher ×0.85, two higher ×0.70, lower excluded unless `allowLowerGrade=true` (×0.60); relevance = gradeFit × (test 30 + TF-IDF text 25 + stack 20 + FSP 15 + industry 5 + freshness 5), inapplicable factors (no stack / no industry in the need) rescaled away; ties by relevance, strength, candidate UUID. Text relevance uses [`TextAnalysis.kt`](../apps/api/src/main/kotlin/ru/fsp/career/matching/TextAnalysis.kt) (RU/EN tokenisation, Snowball Russian stemmer, IT-term canonicalisation, TF-IDF cosine) over the visible narrative only; listed stack tags are scored once by the stack factor (listed + described 0.8, described only 0.5, listed only 0.5 × reliability of the whole list), so keyword stuffing is discounted. Bank mode orders by confirmed-profile strength = 65·test + 25·FSP + 10·freshness. Every result returns `factors[]` with points and Russian texts, `flags[]` and a summary `explanation`; candidates without FSP history get 0 for that factor and an explicit «История ФСП не привязана — … это не штраф». Filters: `specialization`, `grade`, `category`, `techStack` (all, alias-aware), `verifiedAchievements`/`fsp`, `industry`, `q`, `limit` (400 on malformed values). Hidden privacy sections are neither returned nor used for ranking. Contacts are never part of match/bank responses; `GET /api/bank/candidates/{id}` returns them only after the candidate accepted an invitation from that employer (single rule in [`ContactDisclosure.kt`](../apps/api/src/main/kotlin/ru/fsp/career/ContactDisclosure.kt)).
- **FSP ID link.** `POST /api/candidates/me/fsp` links a participant ID through the `FspRegistry` interface; the current `MockFspRegistry` reads the synthetic V6 seed (40 participants `*@demo.fsp.local`, 80 results marked «(демо)»). Ownership is proven by equality of the registry e-mail and the caller's verified Keycloak e-mail (a typed ID alone is public); unknown and foreign IDs both return 404; an ID can belong to one account (409). Imported results become `achievements` rows with `source='fsp'` and points by an explicit policy (level × outcome, 1..20); unlinking and consent revocation delete them. These are **synthetic demo records, not official FSP data**.
- Executed here (earlier run, before the adaptive assessment; current assessment checks are listed in the section above): `cd apps/api && gradle test --console=plain` returned `BUILD SUCCESSFUL`; the generated JUnit reports show **8 unit tests passed** (4 validation + 4 resume) without a DB. A second run with `TEST_DATABASE_URL=jdbc:postgresql://localhost:5432/fsp_career` against the local Compose PostgreSQL returned **9 passed, 0 skipped, 0 failed**, including the Flyway/seed/invalid-role constraint integration check; the report was inspected after that run. [`ValidationTest.kt`](../apps/api/src/test/kotlin/ru/fsp/career/ValidationTest.kt) checks input-grade/text validation, salary request deserialization and default invitation contact fields. [`ResumeTest.kt`](../apps/api/src/test/kotlin/ru/fsp/career/ResumeTest.kt) tests extraction of synthetic PDF suggestions, PDF-size/type rejection, career-field validation and Cyrillic PDF export, not HTTP upload/confirm authorization or PDF malware safety. [`DatabaseIntegrationTest.kt`](../apps/api/src/test/kotlin/ru/fsp/career/DatabaseIntegrationTest.kt) checked the 25-question V1+V2 seed count and invalid-role constraint against the local database. These tests do **not** exercise the HTTP assessment/ranking/authorization flows or psychometric quality. A real local database migration/constraint check ran. A host-run Ktor server backed by that database returned `GET /health` 200, `GET /openapi.yaml` 200, unauthenticated `GET /api/candidates/me` 401 and an allowed-origin (`http://localhost:3000`) CORS preflight 200. Separately, [`smoke_e2e.py`](<smoke_e2e.py>) passed against all six running Compose services: it creates short-lived **admin-verified synthetic** users and a temporary direct-grant test client, checks real JWT issuer/audience/role enforcement and rejects a tampered signature or newly issued token missing the API audience, processing/publication consent, an assessment attempt and immediate retake rejection (now the survey + adaptive flow), matching and skill filtering, reviewed multipart résumé import and branded PDF export, pre-acceptance contact masking, accepted-invitation contact release, and consent revocation/removal from matches. - Automated checks for matching (executed for this change, `gradle test` with a throwaway PostgreSQL 16 so V1, V2 and V4 (now V6) applied): unit tests for the NLP pipeline (Snowball reference words, tech-term canonicalisation, TF-IDF), the engine (category gate, grade multipliers, FSP/no-FSP texts and the «no penalty beyond the factor» invariant, stuffing discount, privacy, filters, deterministic ties, bank strength, recommended categories), FSP policy/ID/ownership and profile validation; `MatchingIntegrationTest` (registry seed counts, mock lookup, idempotent import, privacy, invitation contact method and disclosure) and `MatchingHttpTest` (the real Ktor module with RSA-signed JWTs served as JWKS: link/unlink, 400/403/404 paths, need selection with factors and categories, bank, card, privacy, invitation contact method, contacts after acceptance, consent revocation). [`smoke_ranking.py`](<smoke_ranking.py>) was rewritten for the new response shape (same Compose prerequisites as before; not re-run in the agent environment): FSP link via a temporary synthetic registry participant owned by the candidate's verified e-mail, explanations and flags, filters and 400s, bank/card, privacy, UUID ties of equal profiles, invitation contact method and publication withdrawal. DB-inserted outcomes remain **fixtures, not evidence** that the assessment scored responses or that FSP verified awards.
- **Offline ranking evaluation (synthetic labels).** `cd apps/api && gradle validateMatching` generates five held-out synthetic markets (360 candidates each, 24 Russian vacancy-like needs; hidden competence/skills/industries, noisy observations including keyword stuffing and absent FSP history) and writes [`validation/matching-results.md`](validation/matching-results.md) (+ JSON, the labelled `matching-pairs.csv` and `matching-candidates.csv`). Labels are computed from hidden attributes, not by experts; see that report for P@5/P@10/nDCG@10/MRR with bootstrap CIs, baselines (random, keyword/self-reported stack, test-only, bank strength), ablations and weight sensitivity.

## Broader isolated functional evaluation (beyond the completed smoke run)

1. Use a **throwaway PostgreSQL database**, API and Keycloak realm, never real people or production data. Pin repository revision, JDK/Gradle/PostgreSQL versions, DB migration checksum, time zone UTC, API configuration, issued synthetic role-bearing tokens and execution time. Set `TEST_DATABASE_URL`, `TEST_DATABASE_USER`, `TEST_DATABASE_PASSWORD` for the existing migration test, then run `cd apps/api && gradle test --console=plain`; record passes/skips/failures separately. API tests require a running JWT issuer with correct `iss` and `aud`; do not bypass auth or publish access tokens.
2. Create distinct synthetic identities: candidate **A** (`backend`, industry `fintech`, opted in), candidate **B** (`backend`, `fintech`, opted in), candidate **C** (`backend`, not opted in), candidate **D** (`frontend`, opted in), candidate **E** (`backend`, opted in, no assessment), and employer **H** (`backend`, `fintech`, minimum `junior`). Set `processingConsent=true` for A/B/C/D/E; grant `consentPublication` only where explicitly opted in. Give each a nonblank synthetic name; keep contact addresses fictitious and private. Record UUIDs locally, never in public result dumps. Establish completed attempts via legitimate API calls in the isolated instance; because the draw is random and server holds the correct key, record **actual** drawn question IDs and resulting score, rather than assuming a particular draw. For deterministic boundary tests, fixture preparation may insert completed synthetic attempts into test DB in a transaction, with explicit `submitted_at`, `specialization`, `score`, `grade` and five question IDs, solely in that isolated DB; separately validate the HTTP scoring path with normal API submissions. Do not call DB-inserted outcomes evidence that the assessment correctly scored responses.
3. **Grade/retake matrix:** covered by `AssessmentFlowDatabaseTest` and `smoke_assessment.py` (all-wrong senior attempt → not passed, junior floor, only middle allowed; all-correct middle attempt → middle confirmed, senior blocked for 90 days; expired attempt closed as failed; foreign/invalid/out-of-order answers rejected). For score-boundary cases inject completed attempts into the isolated DB only and label them as fixtures.
4. **Ranking matrix:** prepare same-specialization assessed A/B with equal scores, texts, stacks and industries; verify tie order by UUID. Link A to a synthetic registry participant owned by A's verified e-mail: expect A first with a positive `fsp` factor and B with `fsp` 0 and the flag «Нет истории ФСП» (the relevance gap must equal A's FSP points — no extra penalty). Vary required grade: exact fit ×1.00, one higher ×0.85 with the overqualified flag, lower excluded unless `allowLowerGrade=true` (×0.60). C (not published), D (other specialization unless `specialization=frontend`) and E (unassessed) must never appear. Check `techStack=Kotlin,SQL` requires both skills irrespective of casing and aliases, `verifiedAchievements=true` excludes B, `category=backend:middle` in the bank returns the exact category ordered by strength, and malformed `grade`, booleans, `limit`, `category` or 11 stack items return `400`. Self-reported `grade`/`score`/stack in profile JSON must not create a category; a long list of tags not described in the experience must receive a visibly discounted stack factor. Hide `fspAchievements`/`fullName` in privacy settings and confirm the card shows «Кандидат №…», no achievements, and the FSP filter excludes the candidate. Capture complete responses (factors, flags, categories) for the record; `limit` truncates the list but `total` reports all matches.
5. **Privacy/security:** bearer absent, bad signature, expired token, wrong issuer/audience, wrong role, foreign candidate attempt, foreign invitation, and candidate-as-employer should fail without data disclosure. Check that `/api/matches` never contains email/phone and that invitation GET/POST returns null contacts before acceptance; only an invited candidate can accept and then both authorized parties can see contact. Review rejection/viewing transitions and logs/errors for leakage. Check email ownership separately: the API derives candidate email from a Keycloak token with `email_verified=true` and rejects mismatched nonblank profile email; test both verified and unverified tokens. Check `PUT` without processing consent fails, and independent publication withdrawal removes discovery without erasing a stored profile. For PDF import, test upload without consent, wrong owner/expired/already-confirmed draft, malformed/encrypted/oversize/non-text PDF and parsed email spoofing; verify only reviewed fields are applied, with no email/publication changes. Delete processing consent and verify profile fields cleared, attempts/history, draft and achievements erased and invitations absent for both parties; verify no match or new invitation remains. This deletes application rows but does not establish complete erasure of backups, Keycloak account, logs or downstream copies; audit those separately. Local Mailpit handles only synthetic development verification, not proof of delivery to real mailboxes or a production security certification.
6. Store a redacted run manifest (commit/revision, migration digest, fixture SQL digest, UTC dates, environment versions), HTTP status/response assertions, test report counts and anonymized ranking output. Destroy the throwaway database after review. If result differs from expected, preserve failing fixture and classify implementation bug versus oracle error. Never publish answers, tokens, contact data, or a real candidate ranking.

## Independent quality study before any consequential use

**Assessment quality (content, discrimination, reliability, decision consistency).** The synthetic study and the real-data calibration plan are in [`validation/assessment-results.md`](validation/assessment-results.md) (sections 5–9). Before consequential use: a pilot with linear forms and anchor families, an IRT fit per specialization, instance-level variance per family, a blinded expert-labelled sample to check agreement with the reference grades, DIF review, and continuous drift/leak monitoring per family.

**Top-k expert/pair evaluation.** The synthetic version of this protocol is implemented and reported in [`validation/matching-results.md`](validation/matching-results.md); its metric code (`RankingMetrics.kt`) is reusable unchanged with expert labels. For a real evaluation, for each held-out employer need have independent domain judges label *all* eligible consented candidates (or a predefined probability sample covering retrieved and unretrieved candidates) for relevance 0–3; adjudicate disagreements (weighted kappa). Freeze system version, `k` (5 and 10), candidate pool and exclusions before labels are revealed. Report `precision@k`, `nDCG@k`, MRR and recall of the category gate, macro over needs with bootstrap CIs, against the same baselines and ablations; never treat unjudged candidates as irrelevant. Separate offline agreement from hiring utility (invitation acceptance by rank) and fairness. **No expert judgments exist in this repository; the reported numbers use synthetic labels.**

## FSP integration boundary (mock implemented, real connector proposed)

Implemented: `FspRegistry` interface ([`fsp/FspRegistry.kt`](../apps/api/src/main/kotlin/ru/fsp/career/fsp/FspRegistry.kt)) with `MockFspRegistry` over synthetic seed tables; `candidate_profiles.fsp_id` (unique) and `fsp_linked_at`; `achievements` gained `source`, `external_id` (unique per candidate and source), `event_name`, `discipline`, `event_date`, `level`, `place`, `result_role`. Import is replay-safe (replace by source), unlink and consent revocation remove imported rows; the points policy is versioned in code (`FspPointsPolicy`) and recency is applied at ranking time. Proposed for production: replace the e-mail-equality ownership check with OIDC account linking to FSP ID (Keycloak identity brokering — the `fsp_id` claim of an FSP-signed token is the proof), call the registry API with a service account, add revocation refresh (`revoked_at`, periodic re-sync), immutable provenance/audit events and an appeal/correction flow; distinguish participation, prize and qualification exactly as the registry defines them and obtain FSP approval for the use of its marks.

## Decision and limitations

The current prototype supports demonstration of intended mechanics, **not** a validated selection instrument. The assessment (`validation/assessment-results.md`) and the ranking (`validation/matching-results.md`) have synthetic validation studies with expert-assigned parameters and synthetic labels; real item calibration, expert agreement, employer outcomes and fairness are not yet measured. Keep synthetic demonstrations separate from user-facing credential claims; require the functional security matrix, expert study and independent held-out ranking evaluation before claiming fitness for recruitment decisions.
